# DONOR USER FLOW

## Purpose

This document describes the complete journey of a donor.

Every developer and AI coding agent should follow this flow exactly.

---

Landing Page

↓

Browse Campaigns

↓

Search / Filter (Optional)

↓

Open Campaign Details

↓

Read Campaign Information

↓

Click Donate

↓

Login Required?

↓

NO → Continue

YES → Login/Register

↓

Enter Donation Amount

↓

Choose Payment Method

↓

Review Donation

↓

Create Payment Session

↓

Redirect To Payment Gateway

↓

Payment Completed

↓

Backend Validates Payment

↓

Donation Stored

↓

Blockchain Record Created

↓

Receipt Generated

↓

Notification Sent

↓

Redirect Success Page

↓

View Donation

↓

Download Receipt

↓

Dashboard Updated

---

## Failure Flow

Payment Failed

↓

Show Friendly Error

↓

Allow Retry

↓

No Donation Saved

↓

No Blockchain Record Created

---

## Cancellation Flow

User Cancels Payment

↓

Return To Campaign

↓

Donation Cancelled

↓

Nothing Stored

---

## Becoming a Fundraiser

A donor may apply to run their own campaigns (Decision 020).

Donor Opens Become A Fundraiser

↓

Submit Application (name, cause, identity reference, contact)

↓

Application Pending

↓

Administrator Reviews

↓

Approved → Role Becomes Fundraiser → Fundraiser Dashboard Unlocked

Rejected → Stays Donor → Notified With Reason → May Re-apply
